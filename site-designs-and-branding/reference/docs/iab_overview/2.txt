Baseline Analytics, Industry Advisory Board Overview
Each Seat Counts 
Baseline Analytics IAB is a world-class Industry Advisory Board consisting of
Executives and Officers who are well respected within their specific industry
segments for technology and/or business achievements. Providing distinct
opinions, seasoned skill, professional guidance, and aid for reach into
consumptive markets. 
Each IAB Seat will represent a select global market segment and an Executive 
from inside that respective field will fill each Seat. Ideal candidates are currently 
working, or recently departed from senior-level positions within the segments. 
The Industry Advisory Board will generally perform the following duties: 
1. Outbound GTM Guidance, Adoption. Under controlled exposure & NDA, 
each Advisor shall be educated to Baseline Analytics company roadmap &
direction. Each Advisor is respectfully requested to provide Industry
connective tissue to aid commercial progress. Adding select Intelligence,
Advisement and Options for Baseline Analytics leadership to consider. 
2. Industry Segment Intelligence. As a collective unit, the IAB will provide 
distinct insights into various market segments and trends according to
Baseline Analytics . The IAB will become guiding Voices for outbound
adoption, strategic rationale and deliver an expert perspective on use case 
consumption and/or deployment trends. 
Commitment 
This Industry Advisory Board (IAB) is a non-fiduciary body with no Baseline
Analytics governance or voting rights, acting solely as an Industry Intelligence
Resource. Thus, zero liability to the Advisor or to their employers. 
Collective attendance at four (4) annual Industry Advisory Board meetings by web 
conferences. Individual Advisors may connect independently (on an as needed 
basis) dependent upon specific charter and topics for advisement.
