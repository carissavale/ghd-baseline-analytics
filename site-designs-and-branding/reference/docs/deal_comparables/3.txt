DATA LICENSING / EQUITY HYBRID
Sportradar + MLB $37.3M equity + annual fees
8-year exclusive deal (2025-2032) | Real-time game data distribution | Cash + equity hybrid structure
WHAT IT IS
Sportradar AG is a global sports data and technology company headquartered in Switzerland, publicly traded on NASDAQ (SRAD). It provides real-time game data,
odds feeds, and integrity services to sportsbooks, media companies, and sports leagues worldwide. For MLB, Sportradar serves as the exclusive distributor of official
real-time game data to the sports betting ecosystem and media partners. This means every licensed sportsbook that offers MLB betting must source its live game
data through Sportradar.
ROLLOUT
The expanded exclusive partnership was announced in February 2025, extending through 2032 (8-year term). Under the deal, MLB received 1,855,724 Class A
ordinary shares of Sportradar, worth approximately $37.3M at signing. Sportradar pays undisclosed annual license fees for exclusive rights to distribute MLB's
real-time game data. This follows the model established across other major leagues where the data distributor pays the league for exclusive access. The deal
replaced and expanded a prior Sportradar-MLB relationship, indicating satisfaction with the partnership structure.
RESULT
The equity component is the most significant structural signal. MLB now holds a direct equity stake in its data distribution partner, aligning long-term incentives. If
Sportradar's stock appreciates, MLB benefits beyond the annual cash payments. The 8-year exclusive term provides both parties with planning stability and justifies
higher capital investment from Sportradar in MLB-specific capabilities.
WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ACIS
MLB will almost certainly expect an equity stake in Baseline as part of any league-wide deal. The Sportradar deal establishes the cash-plus-equity hybrid as MLB's
preferred deal structure. The 8-year duration is a benchmark for contract length. Plan for this in your cap table and term sheet.

DATA LICENSING / EXCLUSIVITY PREMIUM
Genius Sports + NFL ~$120M/yr
4+3 years (2021-2029) | Exclusive real-time data distribution to sportsbooks | Replaced prior Sportradar deal
WHAT IT IS
Genius Sports Limited (NYSE: GENI) is a sports data and technology company that became the NFL's exclusive distributor of real-time data to sportsbooks in 2021,
replacing Sportradar. Genius also acquired Second Spectrum (the NBA's tracking and analytics provider) for $200M in 2021, giving it analytics capabilities across
NBA, EPL, MLS, and WNBA. The NFL deal is a pure data monetization play focused on the legal sports betting market.
ROLLOUT
The NFL ran a competitive bidding process between Sportradar and Genius Sports for the exclusive data distribution rights. Under the prior non-exclusive
arrangement with Sportradar, the NFL received approximately $20M/year. By granting exclusivity and running a competitive process, the NFL drove the price to
approximately $120M/year, a 5-6x increase. The initial contract was 4 years. In 2023, the NFL and Genius Sports extended the deal by 3 additional years through
2029, demonstrating satisfaction with the partnership.
RESULT
This deal established the highest-value data distribution agreement in professional sports. It definitively proved that exclusivity is the single most powerful pricing
lever in league-wide technology partnerships. The 5-6x price increase from non-exclusive to exclusive demonstrates the premium that scarcity commands.
WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ACIS
The exclusivity premium is the critical insight. If ACIS can secure exclusive positioning as MLB's official AI-powered pitcher injury prevention system, pricing should
reflect a 2-5x premium over what a non-exclusive arrangement would command. The ~$120M/yr figure is not directly comparable (it is a betting data play), but the
structural lesson about exclusivity pricing absolutely applies.
