Square: The default option would be to place the 
logo on the bottom right of layout.
Landscape: When working with landscape layouts, 
we prefer to place our logo to the bottom right.
Tall Portrait: If your content is vertically centered in a 
portrait frame, we prefer our logo to also be right 
aligned at the bottom. 
Portrait: Our default option would be to place our 
logo at bottom of layout. Preferably on the right. 
Note: These are the preferred positions. Feel free to 
place the logo on any corner of the layout if its 
deemed appropriate.
